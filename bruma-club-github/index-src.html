Our Story

Why Bruma.

Bruma is the mist that settles over Lima — the garúa. It doesn't announce itself. It simply arrives, and the city knows.

We grew up under that mist, beside the finest cotton on earth. Peruvian pima has dressed quiet wealth for generations — it just rarely carried a Peruvian name.

So we built a club, not a brand. Small editions, numbered by hand, cut in Lima ateliers that already sew for houses you'd recognize.

We don't say much. Neither will you. The piece speaks at the right volume.

Questions

Before you ask.

How does the drop work?+

100 pieces. Four colors, editions of 25, each piece numbered in order of reservation. The first edition was revealed July 18, 2026. When a color sells out, it is never reissued.

When does it ship?+

Orders are dispatched from Lima. Delivery options and estimates will be confirmed when live checkout is enabled; join the list for international shipping news.

What's the fabric?+

Peruvian pima cotton blended with modal — heavyweight, matte, with a drape that falls rather than clings. Flat-pressed seams, a collar that holds its line, and a leather patch embossed with your number.

Can I return or exchange it?+

Size exchanges are accepted within 7 days of delivery, unworn, with the box and passport intact. Numbered editions are not refundable — your number is yours alone. Full details under Shipping & Returns below.

What size am I?+

The cut is relaxed but precise. Check the — and if you sit between sizes, take the smaller one. Pima softens with wear.

What is the membership?+

Owning a piece makes you a member, for life. Your box arrives with a numbered Club Passport, your personal Member No. and an Edition Card. Members get priority access to every future drop before it opens to the public.

What if my color sells out?+

Then it's gone — that's the point. You can reserve another color, or join the list to hear first when Drop 002 is announced. Numbers are never reissued.
